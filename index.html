🚨 What Should I Do?

Quick guidance for common digital emergencies.

🔗 Suspicious Link

Do not click it. Verify the sender and website independently.

📱 Hacked Account

Change the password, enable two-factor authentication and review account activity.

💳 Payment Fraud

Contact your bank or payment provider immediately and preserve transaction details.

📩 Scam Message

Do not provide OTPs, passwords or financial information. Report suspicious messages.
